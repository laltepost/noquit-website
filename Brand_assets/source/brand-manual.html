Section 01

01Brand foundations.

Category

Functional fitness repeatability capsule. Not a pre-workout powder. Not a fat-burner. Not a pump product.

Primary audience (single ICP)

Consistency-seeking functional fitness athletes training 4–6+ sessions per week who want to maintain quality output when fatigue builds and recover ready for the next session. Supplement-literate; they read labels and treat supplements as part of a training stack.

Adjacent audience: serious endurance athletes (runners, cyclists, triathletes). Visual register and copy should lead with functional fitness and not exclude endurance.

Brand register

Brand — design is the product. Marketing surfaces (homepage, PDP, science, about, FAQ) carry the brand; the storefront is itself a brand statement, not just a transactional surface.

Brand promise

Finish Strong. Recover Ready.

Positioning (internal)

NØ QUIT is the phenylcapsaicin-anchored capsule designed to support late-session output and recovery after hard training, so athletes can train hard more consistently.

What NØ QUIT is NOT

  • Not a stimulant "buzz" product.
  • Not a pump-first product.
  • Not a weight-loss / thermogenic brand.
  • Not a product that encourages unsafe training or "ignoring pain."

Anti-references (must never feel like)

  • Supplement-bro hype (Bang / C4 / GNC). Neon greens, ALL CAPS GAINS, before/after photos.
  • Pharmacy-clinical bland (CVS / Centrum). Blue + white, stock-smile models, regulatory tone.
  • Cottage-wellness (Goop / Moon Juice). Pastel + beige, hand-lettering, "rituals."
  • Generic DTC Shopify. Inter on off-white, identical hero + product grid, rounded everything.

Differentiation (one line)

Repeatability + recovery in one clean capsule, with a science moat kept off-pack (QR) to preserve premium branding.

Section 02

02Brand architecture & naming rules.

Visual brand mark (logo)

NØ QUIT, slashed Ø (Unicode U+00D8).

Digital / searchable string

NOQUIT PERFORMANCE — no special characters. Use this for domains, email, social handles, page titles, alt text, SEO.

Writing rules

  • Logo: always write NØ QUIT (Ø). Export as outlined vector for print to avoid glyph rendering issues.
  • In body copy: "No Quit Performance" (Title Case) is acceptable. Avoid Ø in URLs.
  • Never use "CrossFit" or imply endorsement. Use "functional fitness" or "high-intensity training."
  • Keep the product descriptor factual: "Phenylcapsaicin Capsules." Avoid selling the dose as a headline.

Product naming (Route A launch)

Working product name: NØ QUIT Phenylcapsaicin Capsules (60 Capsules).

Front-of-pack promises are the product identity; the ingredient descriptor stays small and factual.

Punctuation conventions

  • Em dashes (—): reserved for metadata only (page titles, review attributions like "Marcus T. — Denver, CO", quotation attributions, footer copyright separators, table NULL cells).
  • In body copy: never use em dashes. Use periods, commas, semicolons, or colons. Density of em dashes is the strongest AI fingerprint in written copy.
  • Middle dot (·): used in CTA constructions like "Shop NØ QUIT · $59" and label compounds like "Subscribe & Save · Billed Monthly."
  • Bullet style: tight, terminal sentences. No trailing em-dash sign-offs.

Section 03

03Messaging, voice & compliance.

Voice

Short, disciplined, premium. No hype. No bro-science. No miracle claims. Spare sentences. Clinical accuracy. No exclamation points. No superlatives. No second-person hectoring.

Allowed intensity language

  • finish strong
  • last reps / late rounds
  • when fatigue peaks
  • repeatable intensity
  • show up ready
  • don't fade

Guardrails (never imply)

  • Disease treatment, prevention, or diagnosis.
  • Pain relief or "numbing" effects.
  • Unsafe training: ignoring injury, ignoring pain signals, reckless "push past anything."
  • Weight-loss promises (burn fat, shred, melt, etc.).
  • Guaranteed PRs or competition outcomes.

Structure / function claim style

  • Supports high-intensity exercise performance when fatigue builds.
  • Supports recovery after intense training.
  • Helps reduce exercise-related muscle soreness (use carefully; avoid "pain relief").
  • Supports repeatable training output across the week.

Required disclaimer

These statements have not been evaluated by the Food and Drug Administration. This product is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

Disclaimer must render at body-text size (13px minimum) and body-text color — never as faded fine print. Place near the claim or in the section footer.

Section 04 — continued

04Color strategy & layout.

Color strategy

Restrained committed monochrome with one accent. Black dominates the surface; steel is the single accent, used at <10% surface coverage. Warm chalk #DDD8D2 is the only off-palette break, reserved for editorial product moments (Pomelli showcase). No gradient text. No glassmorphism as default. No tinted backgrounds outside the listed surface ladder.

Layout principles

  • Minimalism: strong whitespace; labels and pages stay uncluttered.
  • Hierarchy: brand mark first; promise second; ingredient descriptor third.
  • Steel accent is a "whisper," not a theme — use sparingly.
  • Container max-width 1320px on web; section padding 148px desktop / 80px mobile.
  • No card-grid replication: vary section patterns (see Section 08).

Photography & content style

  • Functional fitness environments: rig, chalk, bars, plates, timer. No explicit branded CrossFit signage.
  • Emotion: late-session grit, controlled form, "finish" moments.
  • Avoid body-shaming transformations, injury content, "fat burn" visuals, stock-model smiles.
  • Female and male athletes in roughly equal rotation; performance signals over physique.

Section 06

06Website brief.

Primary goal

Convert consistency-seeking functional fitness athletes by making the promise instantly clear and the science accessible without clutter.

Secondary goals

Build trust (quality/testing) and capture emails for retention/subscription. Pre-launch: capture launch-list signups (live checkout is disabled until launch — see microcopy).

Key pages (v1 site)

  • Home — brand + promise + product hero + 3 pillars + comparison table + trust + reviews + FAQ preview + email capture.
  • Product (PDP) — single SKU, subscribe / one-time, supplement facts, benefits rail (horizontal scroll), reviews, FAQ, shipping.
  • Science — QR destination + on-page: compound, observed effects, what it means / what it doesn't, protocol (step-flow), quality.
  • About — brand story, three principles (editorial poster), four standards (numbered grid).
  • FAQ — categorized, sidebar navigation, contact block.

User journey

  • Ad / creator content → Home or PDP.
  • Hero promise → supporting bullets → social proof → trust markers → buy.
  • Optional deep dive: Science page (factual summaries, no hype).
  • Post-purchase: email onboarding → science → community habit.

UX principles

  • One screen to understand: what it is, who it's for, why it's different.
  • Minimal page weight; fast load; mobile-first.
  • Use "supports" language; avoid prohibited claims.
  • No CrossFit references. Use "functional fitness / high-intensity training."

Tech stack (current)

  • Static HTML + CSS + vanilla JS (no framework, no build step). 5 pages, shared styles.css, shared main.js.
  • Fonts via Google Fonts CDN (Barlow Condensed, Barlow, DM Serif Display).
  • Pre-launch: cart drawer renders a disabled Checkout button reading "Checkout opens at launch."

Recommended at launch: Shopify or Stripe Checkout for transactions; Klaviyo for email; GA4 + post-purchase 1-question survey.

Accessibility floor: WCAG AA — see Appendix C for full spec.

Section 09

09Creator & social toolkit.

Content pillars

  • Finish moments (late-session grit, clean form).
  • Repeatable week (consistency identity, streaks).
  • Recover ready (next-session readiness).
  • Minimalism (no shaker, no drama, no hype).
  • Proof (QR science page; factual).

Creator do's

  • Use functional fitness settings without trademarked signage.
  • Speak to consistency: "train hard today, show up ready tomorrow."
  • Use support language (supports performance / recovery).
  • Include disclosure (#ad, #sponsored) when required.
  • Periods, commas, colons. No em dashes in body copy.

Creator don'ts

  • No disease claims, no pain-relief claims.
  • No fat-loss promises.
  • No "ignore injury / ignore pain" messaging.
  • No CrossFit references or implied endorsements.
  • No "EXPLOSIVE GAINS" caps copy.

Sample 15-second script (UGC)

"The last reps are where I usually fade. NØ QUIT is built for repeatable intensity. Supports performance when fatigue peaks. Supports recovery after hard sessions. Finish strong. Recover ready."

Note the periods. The brand voice gets its weight from stop punctuation, not em dashes.

Section 10

10Appendix & changelog.

A. QR 'Science' page draft (plain-English)

Headline: The Science Behind NØ QUIT

NØ QUIT is built around phenylcapsaicin and designed for repeatable high-intensity performance and recovery after hard training.

What researchers observed in trained functional fitness athletes:

  • Improved post-session squat performance (load and repetitions).
  • Reduced decline in countermovement jump performance.
  • Maintained weightlifting performance across rounds.
  • Lower delayed-onset muscle soreness at 24h and 48h.

B. Substantiation checklist

  • Maintain a claims library (every claim tied to a specific outcome and evidence).
  • Keep PDFs of all human studies and internal summaries (one page each).
  • Keep COAs and third-party testing reports for each lot.
  • Creator compliance: review scripts for banned claims before posting.

C. Accessibility floor

  • WCAG AA. Body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1 (current --c-text #A8A39C on #080808 = 5.5:1, pass).
  • Full keyboard navigation across cart, gallery, accordion, purchase toggle, forms.
  • prefers-reduced-motion: reduce respected: scroll-reveal animations disabled, content visible immediately.
  • Descriptive alt text on all product, brand, and editorial images.
  • Color never the sole carrier of meaning (e.g. subscribe vs. one-time, error states).
